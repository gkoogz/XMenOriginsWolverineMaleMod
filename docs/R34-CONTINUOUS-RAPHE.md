# R34 continuous external raphe — local candidate

Extends a narrow rounded midline relief over the scrotal surface and onto the controlled external perineal/pelvic patch. Fairs the abrupt proximal ventral endpoint. Surface-normal sampling is spatially smoothed, shared body/graft seam members receive identical displacement, and triangle-orientation guards limit aggressive edits. Normals are regenerated in the altered region. The head/crown positions are unchanged in the verified saved-settings case.

Validation: 12 deterministic cases, including exact screenshot controls and saved settings; finite geometry, no new flipped faces in the 10 main stress cases; replacement drawing, stream/index restoration, and device reset/recreation passed. Exact screenshot and saved settings show zero edited-region BVH intersections. This does NOT establish collision freedom across sliders or animation: pre-existing proximal shaft/pouch intersections remain in other cases, and several cases gain additional contacts (see bvh-audit.json). The correction is conservative in compressed poses. No medical-validation claim.

Only d3d9.dll is replaced. Saved settings and the character package are preserved. The game is not launched automatically. Use Rollback-R34.ps1 with the game closed to restore the backed-up R33 runtime. This is a local candidate, not a published release.
