# Oblique rounded crown and continuous raphe

Extends crown-raphe-meatus without replacing the accepted smooth primary form. The ventral coronal edge advances toward the tip while the dorsal edge stays in place, giving an oblique margin. The distal cap lengthens smoothly by up to 1.02 model units. The existing terminal recess travels with the cap. These units are not physical measurements.

The rounded shaft raphe increases from a maximum relief of approximately 0.38 to 0.58 model units. A smoothly tapered center strip bridges the former transverse coronal lip into the frenular region, retaining the lateral rounded crown shoulders. Local surface fairing removes the short highlight at the end of this blend. The narrow central frenular attachment uses a longer size-blend than the lateral crown; this prevents the original short scaling mask from reintroducing a crosswise lip at large sizes. The meatal slit remains a closed divot, not an internal urethral lumen.

Glans Size 50 now means unscaled 1.00x, the previous minimum/default geometry size, with zero adjustment. The new range is 0 = 0.85x, 50 = 1.00x, 100 = 1.60x, piecewise linear. The lower endpoint is limited to a modest reduction to preserve the attachment transition. An early 0.60x trial reversed ring order and was discarded. The preview has five rendered settings; Blender has continuous control. Both before and after use the new physical-scale mapping, so a midpoint comparison is 1.00x against 1.00x. The OBJ is exported at slider 50.

Topology is unchanged from the previous delivered mesh: 10,554 vertices / 21,086 triangles, including the previously repaired raphe seam. Forty-one control samples pass finite coordinates, nondegenerate faces, ordered body rings and uniform scaling of the fully weighted crown. Terminal rings are excluded from the axial ordering assertion because the closed meatal indentation intentionally turns inward. No open or nonmanifold edges in the edited region. BVH checks at five sizes find no nonadjacent intersections involving edited faces. Blender before/after evaluated coordinates are checked against their own expected samples. See audit JSONs for measured errors. These checks do not cover animation or physics.

The comparison provides 120 images: two models, five sizes, four views, three shading modes. The previous artifact is preserved. Source scripts are in sources/ and require the existing workspace inputs and mesh_data.py; model-data.json and the Blender file contain the actual new geometry.

This is an authoring model; game integration, final UV transfer and rig/physics transfer remain incomplete. The game was not launched or modified. The shape is not medically validated. Some retained source-shaft faceting remains outside the newly sculpted transition.
